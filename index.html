關於這個圖鑑About this Pokédex

做給小女兒的寶可夢圖鑑 A Pokédex made for my little girl

我個女好鍾意寶可夢。與其買多一件玩具，我想同佢一齊做一樣屬於我哋嘅嘢：一個可以一齊睇、一齊讀、一齊玩嘅圖鑑。

My little girl loves Pokémon. Instead of buying another toy, we built something of our own — a Pokédex we can look at, read and play with together.

所以每張卡都有英文名同香港譯名並排，仲可以用語音讀出來。佢可以看圖認字，我可以陪佢讀英文；猜謎遊戲就係我哋嘅複習時間。

Every card shows the English name next to the Hong Kong Chinese name, and both can be read aloud. She learns characters from the pictures and sounds while we read the English together. The guessing game is our revision time.

呢個專案唔牟利，以開源方式分享。你可以自由下載、修改，同你嘅小朋友一齊用。

This project is non-commercial and shared as open source. You are free to download it, change it, and use it with your own kids.
